## Operator frequency (from doctrine + taste)

You watch longer when someone is **doing a difficult thing carefully** — barista, craftsman, runner, chef, pilot, detective. Competence under load is truth; hard to fake.

**Reverence without performance:** people taking something seriously without making a spectacle of themselves.

| Shows up in | Examples |
|-------------|----------|
| Film | *Le Cercle Rouge*, Melville, restrained crime, conduct on screen |
| Places | Kenyan camps, Japanese architecture, listening rooms, Zen spaces |
| Social | Long-form conversation, serious amateurs, ballast characters (Joanna Hoffman tier) |
| Tags (recommendations) | Ensemble pressure, institutional patience, inference over exposition, ballast dynamic |

**Not:** hype, explanation, forced lesson, obvious symbolism, talking down to the audience.

## Film as Sunday nourishment

Sunday criterion applies: *Does this activate a dormant room without spending envoy budget on wrong company?*

A film can be **frequency** — same as one excellent conversation or one museum hour.
