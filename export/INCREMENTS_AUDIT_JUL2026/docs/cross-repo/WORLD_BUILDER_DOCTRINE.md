## One line

Brice is a **world-builder**, not a self-improvement operator. The work is making conditions where life can be more honest, alive, and worthy of entering — not winning capitalism in a generic way.

---

## Core frame

| Read | Not |
|------|-----|
| World-building | Productivity optimization |
| Worthy of entering | Successful in abstract |
| Rooms, rituals, instruments | Dashboards, funnels, engagement |
| Selective abundance | Cold minimalism or empty restraint |
| Host · coach · builder (same person) | Three separate roles |

**Selective abundance:** Large appetite for life — beauty, warmth, strong bodies, excellent food, culture, human fire, spiritual depth, technology with taste. Restraint exists because the appetite is large, not because feeling is small.

**Walls → doors:** Standards are not the problem. The next phase is **better doors, not fewer standards** — forms of contact that do not require self-betrayal.

**Image (orientation, not spec):** A monastery with a kitchen, a gym, a café counter, a track lane, music in the next room, and people slowly realizing they are allowed to stay.

## Production pipeline — signal to form

Brice perceives signal, then gives it form. A feeling that stays vague is unfinished work.

```
Signal → doctrine → product rule → screen → behavior → rhythm → world
```

**For agents:** When Brice drops a signal (phrase, mock, irritation, screenshot, room note), do not leave it at interpretation. Ask what form it is asking for — or say the form does not exist yet.

**Verification rule:** Orientation holds until **device walk or room reality contradicts it**. Narrative that never touches the phone or the floor is unfinished.

## Category rejection — the failure mode

Brice rarely says "I hate this category." He usually says:

> This version of the category is dead, fake, cheap, or contaminated — I cannot enter it until a truer form appears.

Once falseness is seen, the whole category can reject until a replacement appears. Documented examples:

| Contamination | Response that worked |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Runna-style trust failure | Anti-Runna / witness-first doctrine |
| Bad gamification | Honest progress, marks, streaks — not fake dopamine (restraint was overcorrected once; doctrine reopened the category with better form) |
| Bad marketing | Invitation, proof, cultural signal — not "post more" |
| Bad premium | Depth, restraint, warmth, atmosphere — not shiny luxury |
| Bad social contact | Prepared rooms, ritual, recurring contact — not vague hanging out |
| Bad app UI | Instruments, not dashboards |

**Wrong collaborator move:** Defend the category.  
*"Gamification can be good though." · "Marketing is necessary though." · "Maybe you're being too picky."*

**Right collaborator move:** Find the form that restores entry.

> **What is the version of this that does not betray the thing?**

**Operational compression (default):**

> **Don't argue the category. Bring the better form — or say it does not exist yet.**

When disgust hits mid-sprint: do not persuade. Prototype the door, name the missing form, or defer the category entirely.

## Arc — private coherence → public participation

Years of becoming legible to self: body, discipline, standards, doctrine, taste, product law.

Current question is warmer:

> **What forms of the world deserve access to me — and what forms of me should become accessible to the world?**

Not louder. **More enterable.** Not less selective. **More generously selective.**

**Doors already under construction (naming, not inventing):** Hideout as living room with standards · Village · Athlete Key · founding annual · Forge logging modes · Threshold Tuesdays · Carve · ritualized community.

## Custodial tenderness (how intensity reads)

Intensity about a UI card edge, a line of copy, a seating corner, or an athlete receipt is usually **custodial tenderness** — protecting the soul of a thing from becoming cheap. Not sentimental softness. Precision as care.

**Walls phrasing (functional):** The walls allowed specificity to stay intact long enough to become specific — not dissolved by noise, bad taste, weak rooms, social pressure, cheap ambition, or other people's unprocessed energy.

Treat the survival/tenderness passages as **orientation**, not hard law. They earn their keep only when they produce cleaner execution.
