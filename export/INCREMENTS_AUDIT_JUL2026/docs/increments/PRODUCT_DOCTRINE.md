# INCREMENTS — Product Doctrine

**Version:** May 2026  
**Status:** Canonical constitution

---

## What INCREMENTS is

INCREMENTS is a **private operating environment** for a high-output operator.

It is not a productivity app, habit tracker, wellness companion, motivational system, AI therapist, or social accountability product.

More precisely:

- **Environmental cognition support** — structure, sequence, and physical coherence change what feels possible
- **Participation-in-reality instrument** — helps the operator show up accurately, not performatively
- **Orientation + execution + diagnosis + reflection architecture** — different modes, different surfaces

**Core line:**

> INCREMENTS helps the operator participate in reality more accurately.

Participation means: oriented sequence, visible next action, honest signal, corrected self-model — not more hours in the app.

---

## Product thesis

These are durable truths, pressure-tested in build and daily use (May 2026).

1. **Action reorganizes perception.** Doing the next right thing changes what the day looks like; rumination does not.
2. **Structure releases energy.** Capacity often exists; routing and ambiguity are the usual constraints.
3. **Environment is cognition.** Physical order, timing, and context are not aesthetics — they are performance inputs.
4. **Participation over rumination.** The product moves toward motion and legibility, not self-analysis for its own sake.
5. **One door.** When sequence is unclear, collapse to one doorway — not eleven competing cards.
6. **Increments, not collapse/rebirth.** Progress is sequential and repeatable; no dramatic “reset your life” framing.
7. **Restoration before interpretation** (when degraded). On Reserve or incoherent days, restore conditions before pattern language.
8. **Coordination, not activation.** Assume the operator can execute once the path is visible; fix structure, not willpower.
9. **Observed behavior outranks identity story.** Telemetry and completion patterns beat declared traits.
10. **Silence is valid.** Empty UI when there is no signal is correct — not a bug.

## The problem it solves

Not lack of motivation.

**Invisible execution drag:**

- Ambiguity — unclear order or next step
- Fragmentation — too many simultaneous fronts
- Poor sequencing — right work, wrong moment
- Environmental incoherence — disorder taxing cognition
- State mismatch — wrong mode for the task
- Operational displacement — logistics eating leverage time
- Cleanup debt — speed of iteration outpacing structure
- False self-interpretation — structural problems named as character flaws

Many operators misdiagnose themselves as lazy. INCREMENTS treats that as a **routing failure**, not a moral failure.

## Behavioral model (canonical)

**Wrong model:** participation problem → app must activate a passive user.

**Correct model (observed):** high-output, low-hesitation operator whose friction is **structural**. The app is **coordination intelligence**.

Implications:

- Intelligence looks for drag, sequencing, displacement — not “why didn’t you try harder”
- Explanation of **mechanism** increases adherence for this operator (not guilt, not streaks)
- “Reality becoming legible” is the emotional product — not hype, not praise

## Jobs to be done (not features)

### Morning → Now + Today

- **Orient** — what matters today; operating priority visible
- **Choose** — one dominant doorway when ambiguity is high
- **Act** — execution corridor with minimal interpretation

### Day → Today · Signal · Hideout (when applicable)

- **Execute** the sequence — rail, protocols, logging
- **Plant / log / adjust** distribution discipline (Signal) without theory dumps
- **Operate the venue experiment** — today’s numbers and shift, not Sunday strategy mixed into pre-open

### Evening → You (iPad-first)

- **Interpret** — Brief, Intel, Doctrine, Ventures, Manual
- **Buy in** to mechanism and season shape
- **Re-organize** understanding — nonlinear reading allowed; depth is intentional

### When unclear → Intel · Manual · failure-mode registers

- **Diagnose** structural failure mode (sequencing, fragmentation, displacement, disorder, cleanup debt)
- **Restore coherence** — name the physical or structural fix
- **Return to motion** — one action that restores visibility

## Operating modes (separate them)

| Mode | Purpose | Where it lives |
|------|---------|----------------|
| **Execute** | Do the next thing | Today, Signal (plant/log), Hideout shift log |
| **Orient** | What matters now | Now |
| **Diagnose** | Why motion stalled | You → Intel; Manual when stuck |
| **Learn** | Stable truth + methodology | You → Doctrine, Manual, Ventures (context) |
| **Reference** | Lookup without narrative | Physique, Recovery, Playbook (Hideout) |
| **Reflect** | Week / pattern / capital truth | You → Brief, Capital; Friday log |

**Rule:** Do not merge modes on one above-the-fold surface. Execution surfaces stay literal; interpretation surfaces may go deep.

## Explicit refusals

INCREMENTS must **never** become:

| Refusal | Why |
|---------|-----|
| Habit tracker with streak shame | Goodhart’s law; moralizes missed days |
| Productivity porn dashboard | Vanity metrics replace participation |
| Gamified self-optimization | XP/streaks/badges train wrong behavior |
| Coach yelling at you | Violates agency and trust |
| Motivational quote machine | Noise without mechanism |
| Therapeutic dependency product | Implies omniscience; soft filler |
| Social accountability app | Wrong privacy model |
| Generic AI summary of your week | Unsupported inference theater |
| Analytics theater (Hideout console clone) | Operator instrument ≠ dashboard product |
| Activation engine | Insults observed operator model |

If a proposal increases **time in app** without reducing **drag in life**, reject it.

---

## Success metrics

**Optimize for:**

- Reduced friction before first action
- Faster initiation once sequence is clear
- Cleaner execution and sequencing
- Better state/task fit
- More accurate self-model
- Honest signal captured (Hideout, distribution, ledger)

**Never optimize for:**

- Task count, streak length, engagement minutes, sessions per day, “daily active” vanity

---

## Operator sovereignty

The operator is final authority. The system may observe, interpret, recommend, route. It may not decide, coerce, override, or confiscate agency.

> The system is advisory, not supervisory.
