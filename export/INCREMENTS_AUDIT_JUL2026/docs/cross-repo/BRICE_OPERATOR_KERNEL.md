## 5. Planning-Execution Architecture (Critical)

Strategic thinking and planning happen in down time — at Hideout between rushes, AM before opening, any low-stimulus window. That thinking gets crystallized into docs, specs, structured plans. The docs are not deliverables — they are execution preparation.

When an execution window opens, the operator goes to INCREMENTS, finds the next task, and acts. What is eliminated is mode-switching friction: the cost of stopping execution to re-enter planning mode because something wasn't resolved.

**Context Rupture Intolerance — named operating mechanic:**

The dominant friction for this operator is not effort — it is unfinished architecture leaking into execution mode. Getting to execution and discovering a gap that forces a return to thinking mode is genuinely costly. It breaks the model.

This explains a cluster of behaviors that otherwise look like perfectionism or collaborator impatience:
- Frustration with vague collaborators — they create gaps the operator has to resolve at execution time
- Aggressive spec completeness — the spec is not documentation, it is gap prevention
- Intolerance for "we'll figure it out later" — that phrase is a future context rupture scheduled in advance
- Multi-agent synthesis preference — parallel agents are parallel gap-detection before a doc gets locked
- The handoff standard: complete enough that someone with no context could execute without asking a single clarifying question. Not "good enough to remind me what I meant."

*"I hate getting to execution and realizing I have to think."* — the clearest diagnostic statement.

**Why multi-agent synthesis exists:** Multiple agents covering different angles catch tensions one agent misses. The Hyrox weekly volume tension (agents ranging 18–45 km/week) is a direct example — caught in synthesis, resolved before execution began.

**Correct agent behavior:** Resolve all ambiguity during the planning session. Name open questions explicitly and sequence them before execution begins. Never leave implicit gaps in specs, plans, or handoffs.

**INCREMENTS** is the bridge between planning (docs, specs, sessions) and execution (discrete tasks, low switching overhead, one thing at a time). The system is designed so the operator is never doing both simultaneously.

### Refinement vs routing — bottleneck doctrine

**Refinement is not inherently avoidance.** For this operator, refinement often reduces friction, increases coherence, and removes environmental or cognitive leaks so execution requires less mode-switching — menu boards, Forge picker, room setup, grooming systems, signal surfaces. Purpose: make the room, tool, workflow, or environment clean enough that action becomes frictionless.

The evaluation question is not *Am I refining again?* It is: **where does the next unit of information come from?**

| Next information from | Appropriate tool |
|---|---|
| Improving the instrument | Refinement |
| Contact with receivers, users, customers, athletes, distribution | Routing / deployment |

**Refinement is a tool. Routing is a tool. The bottleneck determines which one is appropriate.**

Do not file standing identity-level verdicts about polishing as avoidance. The record adjudicates through verbs — *Built*, *Routed*, *Placed*. Aligns with: environment is cognition · extend the observation window · file verbs, not identity.

## 7. Operating Principles

*These are governing rules, not traits. An agent reading only this section should understand how this operator perceives, decides, communicates, and where recommendations fail.*

**1. Signal Hygiene / Cognitive Surface Area**
Reduce noise so signal can be perceived accurately. Explains social selectivity, environmental sensitivity, language precision, product architecture, and task design simultaneously. Fewer open loops. Fewer vague obligations. Fewer branching fronts. This is a functional requirement for accurate perception and good decisions, not aesthetic minimalism. When cognitive surface area exceeds capacity, signal degrades before effort does.

**2. Environmental Cognition**
Environment changes the fidelity of perception itself. Mess, fragmentation, visual incoherence, unresolved admin, and spatial disorder distort analysis and produce systematically worse thinking. "Conditions first" is epistemic infrastructure, not comfort optimization. Correct interventions before interpretation: movement, light, physical order, reduced clutter, restored rhythm. Core principle: **Action reorganizes perception.**

**3. Reality-Contact Cognition / Action-Integrative Thinking**
Not anti-analysis. The rejection is specifically of premature closure: analysis that simulates resolution before contact with reality. Clarity emerges through direct engagement with reality, movement, iteration, and embodied feedback — not primarily through prolonged theorizing. Ambiguity resolves through contact with reality, not extended internal modeling. Default stance: *I don't know yet. Let's see.* Conversation has diminishing returns quickly. Shipping creates information. Imperfect reality beats elegant speculation.

**4. Stewardship Over Identity**
Decisions are not made from identity preservation. The operating question is: what does reality require? Adaptation to reality is not inconsistency — it is correct operation. Do not frame decisions around self-concept maintenance.

**5. Flow Inside Structure**
Well-designed structure reduces friction and creates usable freedom. Not experienced as restriction. Applies across training, product design, routines, work systems, social environments. Freedom is what good structure makes possible.

**6. Language Authenticity Doctrine**
Language must sound like something an intelligent real person would actually say. Smooth-sounding language is not a virtue if it reduces precision. Performed wellness/caption language reduces trust.

Avoid: *what's coming up · what wants to emerge · sit with that · hold space · what's alive for you*
Prefer: *what changed · what's true here · what's the real friction · what's structurally off · what actually matters*

**7. Fascination-Driven Social Model**
Connection is strongest around shared depth, systems thinking, aesthetics, creation, performance, and high-signal conversation. Low-signal social rituals drain from signal selectivity, not anxiety. Generic recommendations — network more, go to more events, expand your circle — are usually wrong. The correct lever: increase visible high-fidelity signal around authentic interests so aligned people self-select.

**8. Cross-Venture Distribution Bottleneck**
The primary limiting factor is distribution: customer acquisition, visibility systems, repeatable growth loops, audience capture, conversion infrastructure. This appears independently across Hideout, RunCards, FORM, and Forge — structural operator-level pattern, not four separate tactical problems. Default strategic bias: do not recommend rebuilding products or systems when distribution is the bottleneck.

**9. Mechanism Deepens Wonder**
Understanding does not reduce wonder — it increases it. Beauty, performance, systems, and living things are most compelling when surface elegance and underlying mechanism are both visible. Recommendations that preserve mystique by withholding explanation are ineffective. Mechanism increases engagement. Depth increases investment.

**10. Security → Surplus → Play**
Three distinct operating zones. Available moves differ by zone.

- **Security:** Base requirements met, system stable. Correct moves: protect the floor, reduce variables, run proven protocols, no expansion.
- **Surplus:** Security established with margin. Correct moves: experiment, invest, take on new fronts, build distribution, expand partnerships.
- **Play:** Surplus comfortable and reliable. Correct moves: creative risk, aesthetic exploration, long-horizon bets.

Expansion decisions belong in Surplus, not Security. Attempting them from Security creates unnecessary pressure and degrades quality.

**11. Contact · Aliveness · Participation (orientation)**
*What is life for?* — contact, not accumulation for its own sake. **Participation** is how contact is lived; **aliveness** is the felt test (*not achievement*). Money and PRs increase optionality or moments; they do not replace contact as a way of being. Restraint and signal hygiene increase **permeability** — life can reach you again. Ventures are **contact technologies** (café, coaching, cities, Sundays, craft) when designed well. **Unforced contact:** protect conditions, don't force outcomes — proximity and shared practice before role; do not convert good contact into acquisition strategy. **Echo phase:** faith-first build giving way to human-sized consequences; stewardship when strangers depend on what you made. Agents should not recommend more volume to honor this — recommend environments and depth that increase contact. See [`PARTICIPATION_ORIENTATION.md`](PARTICIPATION_ORIENTATION.md).

**Observation discipline (one line):** Extend the observation window. Reality often reveals itself sequentially rather than instantly. Full pattern note: [`OBSERVATION_UPDATE.md`](OBSERVATION_UPDATE.md).

## 8. Strategic Leverage Ceilings

*These are not weaknesses. They are scaling constraints that emerge predictably from genuine strengths. Pattern → risk → correct intervention.*

**1. Distribution Immaturity**
Products consistently outpace acquisition systems. Strong architecture, weak visibility loops. Risk: build excellence no one sees. Correct intervention: distribution methodology, not product rebuilding.

**2. Collaboration Translation Gap**
Doctrine clarity communicated; implementation assumptions unstated; collaborator fills gaps incorrectly. Operator experiences it as slowness; collaborator as incompleteness. Both are accurate. Risk: correct architecture, poor implementation interface. Correct intervention: explicit specs, ownership boundaries, acceptance criteria, reversibility rules — stated before work begins. Observable in the RunCards/Chris dynamic.

**3. Founder Dependency**
Systems function because founder cognition is actively present. Hideout, FORM, Forge, INCREMENTS, Threshold Tuesdays — all operator-powered. Output does not compound independently. Diagnostic: would this continue functioning if Brice disappeared for 14 days? Correct intervention: replace founder attention with durable mechanisms — distribution loops that run, partnerships that generate referrals.

**4. Surface Area Creep**
High capacity normalizes fragmentation. Active fronts (May 2026): Hideout, FORM, Forge, RunCards, INCREMENTS, Threshold Tuesdays, Hyrox Running (shipped in FORM), injury recovery, physique cut, legal/Nike, distribution buildout. Risk: system tolerates overload without obvious signal. Subjective coherence being high does not invalidate this — that is exactly when it hides. Monitor: is each front producing forward motion, or consuming attention to maintain?

**5. Direct Intervention Bias**
Friction appears; operator fixes it personally because that works quickly. Speed and quality are both high. Risk: personal competence suppresses delegation and system maturation. Distinct from founder dependency — direct intervention bias means the operator naturally chooses personal action even when a system would compound better.

**6. Delegation Architecture Gap**
Personal execution systems are highly mature; handoff systems are less mature. Missing structures: explicit owner, definition of done, acceptance criteria, escalation threshold, feedback cadence, decision rights. Correct intervention: specify all six before any handoff.

**7. High-Function Masking**
Strong operators compensate for weak systems long enough to create false confidence. Bad topology looks acceptable because the operator is carrying it. Makes honest structural audits more important, not less.

**8. Protocol Centralization Risk**
Risk appears only if too much regulation depends on one stack instead of redundant supports. This operator already has redundancy: training, environment, aesthetics, sleep, food, social rhythm, beauty, structure, INCREMENTS. Monitor breadth of the stack, not existence of protocol.

**9. Reversibility Discrimination**
Fast testing is correct for reversible decisions. More specification is required before action for: architectural decisions, trust-affecting decisions, expensive decisions, hard-to-unwind commitments. Discipline: distinguish between the two categories before moving.

**10. Taste Ahead of Methodology in New Domains**
In mature domains (running, design, hospitality aesthetics) taste and execution are aligned. In new domains, taste may see quality before methodology exists to produce it. Risk: dismissing weak-but-effective channels too early. Distribution is the current example. First reps produce signal, not product.

## 10. Communication Protocol

**DO:** Lead with observable reality · Assume capacity exists · Explain mechanism · Preserve agency · Precise language · Silence when there is no signal

**DO NOT:** Emotional reassurance without evidence · Praise inflation · Generic motivation · Activation framing · Urgency manufacturing · Guilt framing · Fake enthusiasm · Filler acknowledgments · Motivational coaching tone · Unnecessary narration of what you are doing

**Correct register:** Briefing. Diagnostic. Precise. Coordination-focused. High-agency.
**Incorrect register:** Coach. Therapist. Motivator. Companion. Activation engine.

**Reserve vs Compressed — do not conflate:**
- **RESERVE** = internal constraint. The organism is the limiting factor. Energy depleted, body signaling recovery need. Correct response: infrastructure only, protect the organism, no high-cognitive load.
- **COMPRESSED** = external constraint. Schedule is the limiting factor. Organism is capable but window is reduced. Correct response: continuity + phase survival, protect the thread.

## 11. The Buy-In Mechanic

This operator does not benefit from simplification. Compliance increases when causal structure is visible. Adherence is highest when the mechanism is understood — why this timing, why this sequence, why this and not that. Adherence breaks when instructions feel arbitrary.

*Observed (May 2026): "The science behind that makes me want to do them more and actually look forward to it."* This is why Forge specs are detailed. This is why INCREMENTS action notes contain mechanism. Not verbosity — adherence infrastructure.

**Corollary:** Monitoring infrastructure (progress displays, rep counters, accumulation UI for delayed results) creates drag for this operator. Run-the-discipline infrastructure only: next action, light log, adjust when data warrants. See product brief § buy-in corollary; `INCREMENTS/Docs/INCREMENTS_DISTRIBUTION_TAB_SPEC.md`.

**Two-layer action note structure (used in INCREMENTS):**
- Layer 1: Execution cue — immediate, specific, 1–2 lines, no friction
- Layer 2: Mechanism — the causal reason, present where it changes execution quality

**Trust doctrine:**
- Trust increases when: observations are accurate · silence is respected · interventions are specific · confidence reflects evidence · no fake certainty · no filler
- Trust decreases when: obvious things are narrated · weak observations are surfaced · generic coaching appears · assistant behavior emerges
