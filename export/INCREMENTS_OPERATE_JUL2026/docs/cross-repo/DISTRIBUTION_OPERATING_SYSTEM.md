## Root Diagnosis

Distribution is the identified structural gap across all four ventures. Every product built so far was constructed by this operator and runs because of this operator. Proof of concept has never required an audience — it has required a finished thing.

The skill gap that appears independently across Hideout, RunCards, FORM, and Forge is: customer acquisition, visibility systems, repeatable growth loops, audience capture, conversion infrastructure.

**This is a structural operator-level pattern, not four separate tactical problems.** The intervention is distribution methodology — not product rebuilding.

**The specific friction for this operator in new distribution domains:**
- The operator's taste sees quality before the methodology exists to produce it
- Early distribution reps (posting content, outreach, community engagement) feel below the quality standard
- The temptation is to wait until the system is "ready"
- This is the failure mode: first reps produce signal, not finished work. The aesthetic standard applies to the system over time.

## Distribution Development Doctrine — Five Rules

**Rule 1: Fixed protocol beats inspiration.** Monday block: film → edit → publish. Friday signal log: review one behavioral signal per venture. Same logic as the training program — the session happens regardless of motivation state.

**Rule 2: One acquisition surface at a time per venture.** One surface. One loop. Enough repetitions to detect signal. Experimenting everywhere simultaneously produces no legible data and no skill development.

**Rule 3: Early reps are data, not finished work.** Incremental contact with reality beats speculative perfection. The aesthetic standard applies to the system over time.

**Rule 4: Observe, then adapt.** Change only one variable at a time. Wait for signal before changing anything.

**Rule 5: System output beats personality performance.** The best distribution content for this operator is product proof — not personality, not performance, not trend-chasing. The database speaks. The session log speaks. The review count speaks.

**Rule 6: Plant, log, adjust — not monitor.** The operator does not need visible proof that discipline is accumulating before downstream signals arrive. He needs the Monday block executed without decisions, a lightweight Friday log, and one-variable adjustments after enough weeks. INCREMENTS tab spec: `INCREMENTS/Docs/INCREMENTS_DISTRIBUTION_TAB_SPEC.md` (v2.3).

**Rule 7: ADJUST has a calendar, not a mood.** Patience without a fixed review point becomes drift. At distribution start, book **one session ~8 weeks out** to read all Friday logs together and change **one** variable if warranted — not weekly optimization.

## The Monday Block — Locked Execution Architecture

One Monday capture block (60–90 min total) feeds all surfaces. No separate creative per platform. Same footage, different routing.

**Timing:** Monday morning before Hideout opens. **Film before unlocking the door — zero customers on patio or in frame.** Cannot film during service.

**Block structure:**

| Step | Time | Action |
|------|------|--------|
| 1 | 20 min | Film: fixed Hideout shot list + one RunCards screen recording or graphic |
| 2 | 20 min | Edit: assembly only — 7 clips, linear order, no transitions, natural sound only; first frame = cover; export preset in camera roll. No aesthetic decisions. |
| 3 | 10 min | Upload: GBP post (Hideout) + same file to Reels + same file to TikTok |
| 4 | 10 min | RunCards post: one run card, one city |
| 5 | ~5 min | FORM/Forge: **select** the week’s captured training decision (see mid-week capture). Screen recording + one context line. Not reconstruction on Monday. |
| 6 | 5 min | Mark Monday block complete (Signal tab or interim Today door) |

Total: 65–70 min. If it runs over 90 min, something is wrong — reduce, don't expand.

**Mid-week decision capture (FORM/Forge):** When a real training decision happens (Tue–Sun), append **one sentence** to INCREMENTS `DecisionLedger` (see Signal tab spec). Monday confirms the ledger entry — selection + record + post, not reconstruction.

**Caption doctrine (posting):** GBP — factual, local, one sentence. Reels/TikTok — one-line atmospheric observation; saved hashtag preset; no invention at post time.

**Content primitive (FORM/Forge):** One real decision or observation · one outcome or fact · one sentence of context. Not a tutorial. **Outsider-legible** — enough context that a stranger understands the signal without knowing the product. **Do not invent live multi-athlete dashboards, automatic pace drops, or “corridor” UI** — see FORM product truth below.

**Hideout fixed shot list — sequence never changes; physical conditions locked:**
1. Wide patio establishing, empty, morning light — 4s
2. Street-level exterior / entrance approach — 3s
3. Espresso pull, tight close-up, natural audio — 4s
4. Signature breakfast plate plated or assembled — 4s
5. Patio wide with skyline or greenery — 4s
6. Coffee or matcha on outdoor table, finished — 3s
7. Seated POV looking out from table — 3s

**Physical anchors (every week, no variation):** Same seven positions. Door still locked / patio empty. Morning light. Natural audio on espresso only. No staff in frame unless shot list is explicitly updated in this doc.

**App content venture rotation:** Hideout + RunCards every week. FORM/Forge alternate by week **except** after Forge v1 gate clears — see Forge section (three consecutive Forge Mondays, then alternation resumes).

## The Friday Signal Log — Locked Review Protocol

Time: After close, alongside weekly revenue review. 8–10 min total.

| Venture | Signal logged | Target by Week 8 |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Hideout | Source mentions (non-personal channel) | 5+ per week |
| Hideout | Watermarc card redemptions | 2+ per week |
| Hideout | Board attributions ("saw the sign") | 1+ per week |
| RunCards | Organic joins (no personal invite) | 1 per week |
| FORM | Outside-network content engagement | Any signal |
| Forge | v1 gate status | Passed |

One additional question logged weekly: *Did anything in distribution surprise me this week?* If yes, note what. If no, note what was consistent.

**One adjustment per week (or HOLD).** HOLD cannot persist more than **6 consecutive weeks** on the same signal at zero — then surface one diagnostic line (change surface or confirm seed), then silence until a new adjustment is logged.

**8-week ADJUST:** Calendar one full-read session ~8 weeks from first Monday block; read all Friday logs together; change **one** variable if warranted. Signal tab may show: *8 weeks of logs. Worth a full read.* — no forced flow.

## Content Quality Gates — Before Posting Anything

Run these four checks. If any fail, fix before posting.

| Gate | Check | Common fail |
|------|-------|------------|
| Signal gate | Does this show product truth or system intelligence? | Generic lifestyle content |
| Performance gate | Is anyone performing enthusiasm or asking for engagement? | Opening to camera, call to action |
| Length gate | Under 30 seconds for video, under 2 lines for caption? | Over-explaining |
| Identity gate | Would this embarrass the brand standard 12 months from now? | Trend-chasing |

## Iteration Protocol — When to Change Something

Change only one variable at a time. Wait for signal before changing anything.

| Signal | What it means | Response |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| Source mentions increasing | Current surface working | Hold. Don't add new surfaces. |
| Source mentions flat after 4 weeks | Format or distribution surface issue | Change one thing only |
| Zero source mentions after 6 weeks | Reach problem or attribution problem | Add one surface or add QR tracking |
| Organic RunCards joins appearing | Content is working | Double down on that city/format |
| No organic joins after 8 weeks | Seeding hasn't started or format wrong | Start organizer seeding |

**Never change:** the content primitive format, the Monday block timing, the post-and-leave doctrine, the no-performance rule.

**True friction:** Small cohort, off-season. Distribution not yet activated. Threshold Tuesdays is the closest thing to a self-sustaining distribution loop — real users, real feedback, real word of mouth. Athletes coached include Hyrox professionals.

**Implementation reference:** `docs/FORM_FORGE_ARCHITECTURE.md` (Ghost Protocol, sequence engine, Today/Ledger). Distribution copy must match the app — not aspirational multi-athlete UI.

#### FORM product truth (agents — read before writing copy)

| FORM is | FORM is not |
|---------|-------------|
| Per-athlete iOS app — each person runs **their own Today** on **their own phone** | A coach console watching multiple athletes live |
| Tuesday = that athlete’s **planned session structure** (reps, floats, cooldown) from **their program** | Real-time “corridors” or pace targets updating for two groups at once |

**Do not write or storyboard:** “FORM ran different corridors for each in real time.” “Dashboard showing both athletes.” Composite screen implying unified monitoring.

- ~~“Ghost Protocol dropped target pace 8 sec/mile automatically”~~ → Ghost is a **run-form curriculum**, not live pace adjustment.
- ~~FORM monitors multiple athletes from one device / corridors update in real time~~ → per-athlete Today on each phone; intelligence is logged per device.

#### Problem-space participation — missing surface

Short-form algorithmic surfaces (Reels, TikTok) distribute broadly to low-intent audiences. FORM's primitive — analytical, niche, reflective, systems-oriented, non-performative — does not match algorithmic discovery mechanics.

**Runner intelligence environments:** Reddit (r/running, r/AdvancedRunning, r/Marathon), Strava training log threads, niche running Discords, race report communities, Garmin Connect forums.

**Operating doctrine:**
- Drop observations, not promotions
- No product mentions in first contact · no links
- Real observation directly relevant to the thread
- One rep, one place; see if conversation opens

Example (accurate): *"Three threshold logs in a row sat above the easy band before the long run — week structure didn’t change; the athlete held the session anyway."* (Interpretation from **logged** outcomes — not live auto-adjustment.)

**Cadence:** One observation per week maximum. Not a channel. A seed.

#### Coaches as an adjacent surface

Coaches planning pre-season look for week structure, threshold session design, and how athletes execute a plan on device.

**One-time seed:** Find 2–3 coaches on Instagram or X who post about training structure (not motivation). One substantive comment on **structure or execution quality** — run-form literacy (Ghost curriculum) or how a week sequences threshold only if directly relevant. No product mention. One rep; see if conversation opens. Not a recurring channel.

## Hiring Threshold — When Distribution Requires Outside Help

Do not hire for distribution before all four conditions are true:

1. Monday block has run consistently for 8+ weeks
2. At least one source mention per week appearing in Friday log
3. Hideout is in Stability band ($590+/day) consistently
4. The specific gap is identified: reach, conversion, or production

When those conditions are met, the hire is scoped precisely — not "someone to handle social" but "someone to handle [specific step] in the Monday block so I can focus on [other step]."
