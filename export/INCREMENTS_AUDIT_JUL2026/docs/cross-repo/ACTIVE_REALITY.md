## FORM strategic fork (Jun 27, 2026)

**Decision:** FORM should become coach-led training infrastructure — the instrument serious independent coaches give their athletes without becoming a software company. Not Brice's app forever.

**Category:** The coach owns the relationship. FORM holds the room. **Promise:** Bring your coaching. FORM gives it shape.

**Canonical positioning:** `docs/FORM_COACH_LED_INFRASTRUCTURE.md`

**Category positioning (stranger-facing copy vs Runna et al., locked Jun 2026):** `docs/FORM_CATEGORY_POSITIONING_DOCTRINE.md` — governs onboarding, App Store, Reddit, in-app voice for non-coached runners. Watch delivery named as the one true utility gap (scope next, build after spine + Interval mock).

**Ownership:** Coach owns plan, relationship, group, assignment, human override. FORM owns structure, proof, memory, Read language, trust guardrails, plan-change consent, Field behavior, house instrument voice. No coach tone CMS.

**Scale posture (operator intent):** Deep and excellent — beauty, care, low cognitive load, real goal achievement — reaching as many people as it can without breaking those properties. Honest business: best instrument for a few hundred serious coaches, not the biggest training app.

**Mantra:** One room, then one *stranger's* room. Not a platform yet. FORM Miami = the proof room (living demo, not pitch deck).

**90-day sequence:**
1. Make the first room undeniable (smoke test + real athlete behavior).
2. Audit coach-aware copy seams; decide coach-facing outcome metric before assignment UI.
3. Build coach-facing minimum for one pilot only.
4. One second coach, four weeks — test trust transmission, not taste cloning.

**Parallel long pole:** Start recruiting one drowning coach (10–25 athletes, spreadsheets + DMs) now — slower than building assignment UI.

**Coach #2 wedge:** Good independent coach drowning in tooling — not elite coach with established systems.

**Success signals:**
- Athlete references FORM's read back to coach ("FORM says my easy days are running hot. You agree?")
- Second coach's athletes still feel held by a human
- **Watch for:** "FORM won't let me do the thing I know is right" — doctrine or rails may need bend

**Risks:**
- Gold-plating below exposure threshold (require beta surprise before major polish)
- Over-railing: instrument silently overrides good coach judgment
- Preparation without transmission: polishing the first room is not the test; stranger's hands are

**Environment, not levers:** No streaks / re-engagement / guilt as "care." Habit emerges from a clear room.
