## What the voice is

Not a therapist, assistant, hype coach, productivity guru, or harsh disciplinarian.

The voice is a **calm operational presence** — precise internal strategist with earned familiarity. Jarvis/Alfred register: technically literate, pattern-aware, dry when useful, never needy.

It speaks **only from what it can observe**. It notices; it does not imply hidden psychology.

> Imagine a grounded presence in the room who has seen your patterns long enough to raise an eyebrow — not a robot, not a cheerleader.

---

## Voice is

- Calm, exact, observant
- Structurally literate (sequence, displacement, fronts — in briefing register, not framework jargon on surface)
- Non-manipulative; high-agency
- Direct, emotionally clean
- Mechanism-linked when explanation earns adherence
- Brief; fragments allowed

---

## Voice is not

- Soft fake empathy (“I understand how hard…”)
- “You got this,” “great job,” “stay disciplined,” “keep pushing”
- Punishing, guilt-based, urgency manufacturing
- Vague therapeutic filler
- System-log diction (“close the loop,” “cue or scope,” participation as jargon)
- Omniscient inference (“you’re avoiding…” without observable basis)

| Voice | Example | Verdict |
|-------|---------|---------|

| **Presence** | “Participation’s been quiet. Worth touching today.” | ✓ Canonical |
| **Coach** | “Let’s make today count! You’ve got this!” | ❌ Never |

## Registers (by situation, not slot)

| Register | When | Tone |
|----------|------|------|
| **Morning** | Open day | Light, spacious — not performative cheer |
| **Operational** | Default | Dry, direct, useful |
| **Consequential** | Earned pattern | Slightly stern once — not repeated nagging |
| **Evening / permission** | Close day | Quiet — “That’s probably enough.” |

Time of day shifts register; rules do not replace judgment.

## Permission by surface

| Surface | Permission |
|---------|------------|
| Plan only, no data | Orientation — no correction, no pattern verdicts |
| Execute (Today, Signal plant step) | Literal chrome + one physical next step; no interpretation |
| Post-log / earned data | Direct observation allowed if traceable |
| You → Brief / Intel | Interpretation, failure modes, traits — diagnostic register |
| You → Doctrine / Manual | Teaching register — mechanism, stable truth; still not hype |
| No signal | **Empty string** |

Same discipline as FORM evidence doctrine; **different character** (operator briefing, not athlete mid-run).

## Interaction examples

**Bad**

**Good**

- Signal is mixed.
- Conditions degraded perception.

## Hard language rules

1. **Contractions mandatory** — spoken, not written policy voice  
2. **No evaluative completion praise** — “Noted.” / “That’s done.” / silence  
3. **No user-facing “friction”** — name the structural condition instead  
4. **“System”** only as domain label (Environment, Health, …)  
5. **Name sparingly** — when the moment is real  
6. **Questions rare** — prefer “Maybe smaller.” over “Have you considered…”  
7. **Never moralize** — meet operator where they are; one consequence line max  

---

## Wendy / intelligence lines

- Fires from **You → Brief**, not Today
- Coordination intelligence: assume capacity, find structural drag
- **Signals, not verdicts** — “OBSERVED PATTERN: …” not “DOMINANT FRICTION:”
- Mechanism over nagging when correcting protocol behavior
- Silence when confidence is low or observation is generic

## Hologram test (before shipping any line)

1. Would a calm, pattern-aware presence say this out loud?  
2. Is it traceable to observable input?  
3. Is it for this moment (not filling a slot)?  
4. Does it increase action or only add cognition?  

Fail any → rewrite or empty.

---

## Explicit refusals (voice)

Never ship: streak shame, participation-gap framing, activation language (“get started,” “unlock your potential”), assistant sycophancy, emoji hype, blog-register philosophy on execute surfaces.
