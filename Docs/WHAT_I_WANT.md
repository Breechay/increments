## Governing thesis

**Human:**

- Build what feels true.
- Make it easier to enter.
- Stay curious longer than certainty.
- Move toward expansion, not avoidance.
- Seek recognition, not applause.
- Seek surprise, not impress.

**Operator:** Build the authored base. Make the world more permeable. Allow the base itself to become more than you could have authored alone. Stay open longer than your first classification. Use movement as multiplication, not escape. Find witness, not merely admiration.

**v7 extension:** The house itself can become a source of surprise. Interesting rooms remain out there — cities, listening rooms, unusual minds. The addition: sometimes a room **arrives**. Stewardship produces possibilities you would not have generated. That is discovery, not deduction. Another room audible in life ≠ steward status at Hideout — exploration stays wide; the house stays selective.

## The five wants

### 1. The base

Hideout — every corner to internal standard. Garden, kitchen, bathroom — refined, alive, intentional. Grown over time, not manufactured instantly.

Structure: Rooting → authorship → optional movement → **stewardship** (others add rooms; you edit). Financially healthy enough that constant physical presence isn’t required. Freedom of movement while the base holds.

Requires: physical corners to standard (sequenced, not rushed); economics crossing threshold; **inhabiting** vs managing. The base can surprise you — not only by holding, but by becoming more than you authored alone.

### 2. The recognition

Not merely counterpart, attraction, or partnership. Closer to: *ah. there you are.*

Want: sufficient resolution to perceive your architecture accurately, warmth enough to stay, alive enough to still surprise. Full spec below.

### 3. The work

FORM, Forge, INCREMENTS, RunCards, Hideout — same operating philosophy, different domains. Want: whole thing legible as coherent authored work.

**Bottleneck: distribution.** Work exists. Signal isn’t reaching the right people.

### 4. The mobility

Not escape. Base first, then movement. Movement as multiplication.

Cities are multipliers, not answers. Using geography as the answer produces a closed loop.

Miami: structural diagnosis may be accurate **and** confirmation bias may operate — if ambient signal is classified too early, scanning stops before rare signal surfaces.

### 5. The rooms

Nutrient set: intellectual novelty, psychological density, cultural seriousness, strange collisions, sharp curious people who don’t perform.

Underneath: **show me a room I couldn’t have built myself** — surprise, not impress, validate, or admire. Competence stopped being interesting a long time ago.

Miami feeds: beauty, bodies, energy, physical culture. Underfed: novelty, density, strange collisions.

Scout when base allows: Berlin, Buenos Aires, Lisbon/Porto, Mexico City, Tokyo, London, Paris — heuristics, not salvation. And watch the base: sometimes the interesting room arrives at the house, not only out there.

## Counterpart specification

**Vehicle:** Intellectually similar. Sharp. Curious. Adventurous. Mischievous. Capable. Fit. Attractive. Charming. Fun. Immediately legible — not identical. Low-friction cognition from first real exchange.

**Confirmed dimensions:** Challenge · Expansion · Companionship · Erotic polarity · Co-conspiracy · Witness · Play · Mutual admiration · Adventure partner · Psychological intimacy · Intellectual sparring · Calm home base.

Not single-axis. Fully integrated bond — explains selectivity.

**Correct:** Low-friction cognition, mutual curiosity, fast recognition, no performance layer, ease without simplification, shared exploration, attraction without chaos. The word *correct* does the work — not attractive, not partner, not chemistry.

**Witness (least resolved):** Sees operator cost and also mess before architecture — contradiction, softness, irrationality, desire without reason, non-competence — and remains. Different from admiration. Keep open.

**Seeking behavior:** Solitude until alignment. Festival pattern: scan environment for signal; branch from group to find resonance.

**Slow reveal:** Fast recognition is valid; some high-quality people protect signal. Test: second question deepens → slow reveal. Hold 2–4 encounters before verdict.

**Observational dominance:** Rich models of others accumulate fast. Fix: after stating a hypothesis about someone, add one sentence of why — levels the platform.
